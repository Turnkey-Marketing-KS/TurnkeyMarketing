## Full Turnkey case-study draft

### How ASAP Auto Repair grew monthly revenue and car count

From October 2024 through June 2026, ASAP Auto Repair reported growth in two measures that matter directly to an independent repair shop: monthly revenue and monthly car count. During this documented period, Turnkey Marketing's work included updated postcard routes and DirectTrack Marketing.

#### Starting condition

At the beginning of the comparison period in October 2024, ASAP Auto Repair reported $125,000 in monthly revenue and 240 cars per month. These figures establish the starting point; the current source material does not document a separate client statement about the business challenge that led to the work.

#### Marketing work performed

The documented work included updates to the shop's postcard routes and the use of DirectTrack Marketing. The corresponding Turnkey service pages are:

- [Auto repair direct mail](https://www.turnkeyautomarketing.com/services/direct-mail)
- [DirectTrack Marketing](https://www.turnkeyautomarketing.com/services/directtrack-marketing)

No participating vendor is named in the approved source material, so this draft does not assign work or credit to an outside vendor.

#### Timeframe

The comparison runs from October 2024 through June 2026.

#### Measured outcome

By June 2026, ASAP Auto Repair reported:

- Monthly revenue of $200,000, up from $125,000—an increase of 60%.
- Monthly car count of 340, up from 240—an increase of approximately 42%.
- An all-time revenue high in three of the four most recent months recorded in the approved internal brief.

The figures describe growth during the documented period. This draft does not claim marketing was the only contributing factor.
