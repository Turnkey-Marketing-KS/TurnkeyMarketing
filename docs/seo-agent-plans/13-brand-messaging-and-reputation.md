# Agent brief: branding, messaging, reputation, and social proof

## Opportunity and scope

The qualified keyword set has no reliable measured volume; broad terms such as `brand pros` and `be well known` are misleading. Build only around auto-repair-specific usefulness and the site’s existing messaging, Know-Like-Trust, photography, and review-management content.

## Page ownership

- Proposed pillar: `/resources/auto-repair-shop-branding`
- Existing messaging owner: `/resources/be-the-guide-auto-repair-shop-messaging`
- Existing trust owner: `/resources/know-like-trust-marketing-filter`
- Proposed review guide: `/resources/auto-repair-review-response-guide`
- Commercial bridges: Social Media Marketing and Digital Marketing

## Phase 0 — overlap map

Inventory every section that discusses brand, message, trust, reviews, photography, proof, tone, and customer experience. Assign each concept to one owner page. Do not create the branding pillar until the overlap map proves it adds a distinct positioning/identity framework.

## Phase 1 — refresh existing owners

- Strengthen the messaging resource around clear customer problem, shop-as-guide, proof, and next action.
- Strengthen Know-Like-Trust as a practical review filter for websites, ads, email, social, and direct mail.
- Add contextual links to `/photos`, Social Media Marketing, Digital Marketing, and relevant results.
- Remove duplicated StoryBrand explanations and avoid implying affiliation/certification unless documented.

## Phase 2 — branding pillar, if distinct

Working specification:

- URL: `/resources/auto-repair-shop-branding`
- SEO title: `Auto Repair Shop Branding: Positioning & Trust`
- Description: `Build an auto repair shop brand through clear positioning, consistent visuals, useful messaging, real proof, and a customer experience that matches.`

Required sections: positioning; ideal customer/work; naming and visual consistency; message/voice; proof/reputation; in-shop and online experience; brand audit checklist. This is not a logo-gallery page.

## Phase 3 — review response guide

Provide original response frameworks for positive, neutral, negative, inaccurate, and sensitive reviews. Include escalation, privacy, tone, response-time, and platform-policy cautions. Do not expose customer information or offer legal advice.

## Measurement

Track branded search trends, resource-to-service clicks, review-guide use, consultation assists, and query overlap with existing messaging/trust pages.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each concept has one canonical owner.
- New content is practical and auto-repair specific.
- StoryBrand/trademark references are factual and limited.
- Review templates protect privacy and avoid admissions/promises.
- Metadata, schema, H1, sitemap, images, links, tracking, and responsive QA pass.
- `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint`, and `pnpm seo:check` pass.
