# Agent brief: multi-location and franchise auto-repair marketing

## Opportunity and decision gate

The export reports zero search volume for the dedicated terms, but a qualified multi-location account can have high value and distinct delivery requirements. Do not publish until Turnkey confirms operational capability and proof.

## Dependency and proposed ownership

- Depends on the shared industry-page registry/renderer.
- Proposed pillar: `/industries/multi-location-auto-repair-marketing`
- Proposed support guide: `/resources/multi-location-auto-repair-seo`
- Proposed support guide: `/resources/auto-repair-location-pages`

## Phase 0 — operational validation

Document:

- minimum/typical location count;
- central versus local decision rights;
- account/access governance;
- brand and offer controls;
- per-location budgets and reporting;
- GBP ownership and duplicate handling;
- location-page standards;
- lead routing and phone attribution;
- review management by location;
- approved multi-location clients, proof, and dashboards;
- sales pipeline demand and delivery capacity.

Exit gate: leadership, service delivery, and sales approve the operating model. If not, stop with a capability-gap memo.

## Phase 1 — pillar

Working specification:

- URL: `/industries/multi-location-auto-repair-marketing`
- SEO title: `Multi-Location Auto Repair Marketing`
- Description: `Coordinate auto repair marketing across locations with central strategy, local visibility, brand governance, reporting, and location-level accountability.`
- CTA: `Review marketing across your locations`

Required sections: central/local model; access and governance; location data; GBP/reviews; location pages; campaign localization; lead routing; per-location reporting; proof; process; fit/not-fit; FAQs.

Use real operating detail. Do not describe generic single-location services with plural nouns.

## Phase 2 — local SEO guide

Cover organization/location entities, GBP ownership, categories, review workflows, local citations, internal linking, reporting, and duplicate/conflict handling. Link to the Digital Marketing service and the pillar.

## Phase 3 — location-page guide

Explain when a location deserves an indexable page; unique address/team/services/proof; navigation; schema; canonical handling; and how to avoid doorway pages. Do not publish templated city pages for areas without a real location.

## Phase 4 — account-quality review

Track qualified account size, sales cycle, close rate, per-location performance, delivery load, and implementation blockers. Expand only if the operating model works.

## Acceptance criteria

- Operational capability is approved before indexing.
- Page includes genuine multi-location process and proof.
- No doorway/city-page pattern is recommended.
- Organization/LocalBusiness/Service schema choices match actual entities and visible content.
- Template, sitemap, metadata, H1, links, tracking, and responsive QA pass.
- `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint`, and `pnpm seo:check` pass.
