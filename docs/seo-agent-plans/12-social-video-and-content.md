# Agent brief: social media, video, and content

## Opportunity

Approximately 150 monthly searches remain after excluding generic podcast and unrelated social queries. The existing Social Media Marketing service is the commercial owner. The goal is to make the service concrete and create reusable shop-specific resources without optimizing for vanity metrics.

## Page ownership

- Commercial owner: `/services/social-media-marketing`
- Template/resource: proposed `/resources/auto-repair-social-media-calendar`
- Guide: proposed `/resources/auto-repair-content-ideas`
- Existing supporting page: `/photos`

## Phase 0 — inventory and intent filter

1. Confirm platforms, cadence, approvals, asset collection, ad scope, comment handling, review response, reporting, and limitations.
2. Inventory approved posts, photos, videos, examples, and downstream actions.
3. Exclude podcast-navigation, unrelated Facebook, job, and generic creator terms.
4. Baseline Search Console and service CTA performance.

Exit gate: approved deliverables and example library.

## Phase 1 — refresh the service page

- Keep the service page commercial and specific to independent auto repair shops.
- Explain content inputs, 3–5-post cadence only if still accurate, approvals, platforms, GBP posting, Facebook ads, comments, reviews, reporting, and client responsibilities.
- Show examples by business purpose: trust, people, work, education, seasonal capacity, review proof, and community.
- Add a fit/not-fit section and explain what social cannot fix.
- Link to `/photos`, Retention Marketing, Digital Marketing, and the two proposed resources.

## Phase 2 — social calendar

Working specification:

- URL: `/resources/auto-repair-social-media-calendar`
- SEO title: `Auto Repair Social Media Calendar & Post Plan`
- Description: `Plan auto repair social posts around real work, people, education, reviews, seasonal demand, community, and measurable next actions.`

Provide a reusable monthly framework, prompts, asset checklist, approval workflow, accessibility guidance, and measurement fields. If downloadable, use an accessible file and maintain a useful HTML version.

## Phase 3 — content ideas guide

Combine photo, short video, blog-topic, and GBP-post intent unless evidence supports separate pages. Organize ideas by customer question and business purpose. Link to the photography guide rather than duplicating its shot list.

## Measurement

Track service CTA clicks, resource-to-service clicks, calls/site actions assisted by social, content production efficiency, approvals, and reuse. Do not use follower count as the primary KPI.

## Acceptance criteria

- All examples are approved and auto-repair specific.
- Service scope and paid/organic distinctions are unambiguous.
- Resources add templates/processes, not generic lists.
- Images/video have alt or accessible labels, dimensions, captions where useful, and sensible loading.
- Metadata, schema, H1, sitemap, links, tracking, reduced motion, and responsive QA pass.
- `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint`, and `pnpm seo:check` pass.
