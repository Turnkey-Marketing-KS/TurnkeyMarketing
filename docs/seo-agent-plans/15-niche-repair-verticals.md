# Agent brief: specialty repair verticals

## Opportunity and guardrail

The qualified cluster represents approximately 60 monthly searches across diesel, European, brake, truck, tire, EV, hybrid, engine, and diagnostics themes. Do not mass-produce keyword-swapped industry pages. Choose one pilot based on business evidence.

## Dependency

This agent depends on the approved industry-page registry and renderer from the collision/shared-foundation work. Do not create a second vertical architecture.

## Phase 0 — choose one pilot

Score each specialty from 0–5 on:

- current client count;
- qualified pipeline demand;
- revenue/account fit;
- approved proof;
- internal subject-matter expertise;
- unique services/operations;
- search demand and SERP fit;
- ability to write at least 40% truly specialty-specific content.

Record the decision in `docs/seo/vertical-prioritization.md`. Example candidates from the export are diesel and European repair, but do not select them automatically.

Exit gate: one pilot approved by leadership, sales, and service delivery.

## Phase 1 — build the pilot pillar

Use `/industries/{approved-specialty}-marketing`. Required content:

1. Specialty-owner problem and desired outcome.
2. Customer/vehicle/work mix and geography.
3. Distinct trust signals, terminology, seasonality, and conversion path.
4. Relevant local SEO, paid, retention, social, direct mail, and measurement tactics.
5. Turnkey’s actual process and deliverables.
6. Specialty-specific proof or an explicit statement that examples illustrate method rather than results.
7. Fit/not-fit and visible FAQs.

Do not create a service page for a service Turnkey does not provide.

## Phase 2 — support content gate

After the pillar earns impressions and qualified engagement, consider one support resource for the strongest distinct intent, such as specialty local SEO or customer acquisition. Do not publish separate SEO/marketing/advertising/design pages for every vertical.

## Phase 3 — authority

Require subject-matter review, specialty client approval, relevant association sources, and original examples. Avoid generic AI-generated vehicle facts.

## Phase 4 — scale/stop decision

After at least 90 days, compare lead quality, account value, impressions, engagement, and delivery fit with the core site. Approve the next specialty only if the pilot clears predefined thresholds.

## Acceptance criteria

- Prioritization is evidence-backed and documented.
- Only one pilot launches in the first cycle.
- At least 40% of the copy is unique to the specialty.
- No doorway pattern or city/service keyword matrix is created.
- Template, schema, metadata, sitemap, H1, images, links, tracking, and responsive QA pass.
- `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint`, and `pnpm seo:check` pass.
