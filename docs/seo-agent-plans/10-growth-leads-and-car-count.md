# Agent brief: growth, leads, calls, customers, and car count

## Opportunity and caveat

The export reports no measurable volume for most cluster terms, but the language closely matches shop-owner outcomes. This is an owner-language validation project, not permission to publish speculative pages.

## Proposed ownership

- Diagnostic pillar: `/resources/increase-auto-repair-car-count`
- Support guide: `/resources/auto-repair-customer-acquisition`
- Optional checklist: `/resources/increase-auto-repair-calls`
- Commercial bridges: DirectTrack, Boost Days, Retention, Digital Marketing, and Consulting

## Phase 0 — validate owner language

Use first-party evidence before writing:

1. Search Console query data.
2. Anonymized sales-call notes and consultation questions.
3. Client-success questions and campaign briefs.
4. At least three internal subject-matter interviews.

Separate these constraints:

- insufficient demand;
- poor local visibility;
- low call/form conversion;
- poor phone booking;
- capacity/scheduling mismatch;
- low retention;
- low-value work mix or ARO.

Exit gate: evidence that the proposed language is used by real prospects, plus one canonical owner for each constraint.

## Phase 1 — diagnostic pillar

Working specification:

- URL: `/resources/increase-auto-repair-car-count`
- SEO title: `How to Increase Auto Repair Car Count`
- Description: `Diagnose whether car count is limited by visibility, lead quality, phone conversion, scheduling, retention, capacity, or work mix.`
- CTA: `Diagnose the constraint limiting your car count`

Required sections: define car count and why more is not always better; diagnostic flow; demand; conversion; capacity; retention; measurement; 30-day action matrix; scenarios. Avoid promising that marketing alone will solve operational constraints.

## Phase 2 — acquisition guide

Explain audience, channels, lead quality, cost per acquired customer, booking, first visit, second visit, and attribution. Link to the budget calculator when live. Do not fabricate benchmark CAC or close rates.

## Phase 3 — calls checklist decision

Create a separate calls checklist only if research shows distinct demand and there is useful first-hand material on GBP/ads/website-to-call tracking and phone handling. Otherwise include it in the pillar.

## Internal-link rules

- The diagnostic page sends each identified constraint to exactly one owning service/resource.
- Service pages link back only to the relevant diagnostic section, not with generic `learn more` anchors.
- Results links must match the claimed outcome and explain the documented work.

## Measurement

Track Search Console query emergence, diagnostic-path interactions, service clicks by constraint, consultation quality, and sales feedback on owner-language fit. Revisit title/URL after 90 days if Google uses different vocabulary.

## Acceptance criteria

- The page diagnoses before prescribing.
- No zero-volume term is presented as a proven demand forecast.
- Every recommended action maps to an existing, accurately described service or neutral resource.
- Content distinguishes marketing limitations from operational problems.
- Schema, metadata, H1, sitemap, links, images, tracking, and responsive QA pass.
- `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint`, and `pnpm seo:check` pass.
