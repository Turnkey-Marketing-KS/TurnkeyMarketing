# Turnkey Marketing SEO master plan

**Plan date:** August 13, 2026

**Initial execution window:** August 17-November 13, 2026

**Planning horizon:** 6 months

**Primary data source:** Connected SE Ranking project `12503330`, with U.S. desktop and mobile tracking, Google Search Console data, website audit, competitor tracking, and backlink data

**Website:** `https://www.turnkeyautomarketing.com`

## 1. Executive strategy

Turnkey should not try to rank all 133 tracked phrases at once. The current opportunity is to consolidate Google around a small set of strong pages, move the closest terms onto page one, and build supporting authority before adding new service pages.

The strategy has five parts:

1. Give every important keyword one intended page in SE Ranking.
2. Protect the homepage as the agency/company page and separate it from the services hub.
3. Move the marketing-plan and direct-mail clusters from page two toward page one.
4. Resolve consultant, outsourced-director, and results-page cannibalization.
5. Grow authority to service, resource, and results pages rather than sending nearly every link to the homepage.

The site should publish only what Turnkey can truthfully deliver. High-volume SEO, PPC, website-design, and lead-generation terms remain scope-gated until the business confirms the offer, ownership, proof, and exclusions.

### Content quality standard

Every published resource must include:

- A single primary intent and target URL assignment.
- A unique title, description, H1, and canonical.
- A concise direct answer near the beginning.
- Original experience, examples, screenshots, or approved proof.
- Named author or accountable organization and a visible updated date.
- Two or more internal links to relevant service/resource pages.
- At least one link from an existing page before publication.
- A useful CTA appropriate to informational intent.
- Article and breadcrumb structured data; FAQ structured data only when visible FAQs are genuinely helpful.
