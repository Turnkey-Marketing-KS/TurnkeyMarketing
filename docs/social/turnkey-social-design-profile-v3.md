# Turnkey Marketing Social Design Profile — V3

## Purpose

Build a recognizable social presence that feels useful to an independent auto repair shop owner before it feels promotional. The feed should demonstrate that Turnkey understands how a shop operates, show evidence that the work is measurable, and make the next step feel simpler.

The creative system is designed for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and selective Google Business Profile reuse. The primary feed format is 1080 × 1350 (4:5), with all essential content protected inside a square-safe center area.

## Research summary

### Pinterest references reviewed

1. [Editorial social identity system](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/1106055989763141877/) — useful for its alternating rhythm of photography, short statements, quotes, and editorial covers. The feed is cohesive without making every tile identical.
2. [Cohesive Instagram feed](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/959689001865778382/) — useful for one-idea-per-tile hierarchy, bold type, occasional image crops, and controlled repetition of color.
3. [Editorial newspaper template system](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/928726754431346262/) — useful for texture, framing, and confident headlines. The dense body copy is deliberately *not* being adopted because it is too difficult to scan in-feed.
4. [Concept-led photo composite](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/1688918607277154/) — useful for showing how one unexpected visual metaphor and one headline can carry an entire post.
5. [Automotive social identity](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/1108378158340611638/) — useful for consistent detail crops and a strong feed-wide mood. Its luxury-car tone is not appropriate for Turnkey; the structural consistency is.

### Platform guidance translated into the system

- Meta recommends a single focal point, less text, and visual consistency across campaign assets. Turnkey posts therefore use one dominant message and move explanation into the caption. [Meta photo-ad guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/photo-ad-format)
- Instagram supports single images and carousels, and recommends 1:1 for partnership carousel assets. The v3 masters are 4:5 for feed presence but keep a square-safe content area for reuse. [Instagram format guidance](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/864398265379023)
- Google Business Profile posts can use photos or video with update, offer, event, and action-button formats. Only the posts with customer-facing relevance should be reused there. [Google Business Profile post guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169)
- LinkedIn recommends consistent, useful Page content and supports targeting by professional audience attributes. Turnkey's proof, operator insight, and team posts are the strongest LinkedIn candidates. [LinkedIn Page best practices](https://business.linkedin.com/marketing-solutions/linkedin-pages/best-practices)

## Audience

Primary audience: owners and operators of independent auto repair shops in the United States.

Their likely mindset:

- Marketing still lands on their desk even when vendors are involved.
- Empty bays create urgency, but random promotion creates waste.
- They distrust vanity metrics and vague agency language.
- They want a plan, accountability, and fewer moving parts to manage.
- They respond to specific shop realities, real numbers, and people who understand the industry.

## Positioning

### Brand role

Turnkey is the experienced outside marketing department that understands the shop, owns the plan, coordinates execution, and reports what should happen next.

### Social voice

- Direct, calm, and useful
- Shop-literate rather than marketing-jargon-heavy
- Confident without hype
- Specific without overexplaining
- Human, practical, and occasionally wry

### Visual north star

**Independent-shop documentary × modern editorial field guide.**

The documentary side supplies real people, real environments, imperfect texture, and evidence. The editorial side supplies strong hierarchy, disciplined whitespace, intentional crops, and a recognizable system.

## Non-negotiable creative rules

1. One post has one job.
2. Most on-image headlines use 3–9 words; 14 words is the hard maximum.
3. A viewer should understand the post in two seconds without reading the caption.
4. The caption carries the explanation, nuance, and CTA.
5. One visual focal point per asset: a face, a metric, an artifact, or a short headline.
6. The logo stays small. Recognition should come from the system, not oversized branding.
7. Proof posts use only supported claims and include a visible results-vary qualifier.
8. Not every post sells. Direct conversion posts are limited to roughly 20% of the set.
9. No generated data, fake dashboards, fake testimonials, or AI-created people presented as clients.
10. AI-generated photos may fill a specific documentary gap only when they are clearly generic scenes and do not imply a real Turnkey client result.

## Visual identity

### Color roles

| Color | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Turnkey navy | `#232E66` | Authority, dark campaign anchors, proof framing |
| Deep navy | `#1A2352` | High-contrast conversion and brand posts |
| Key blue | `#3F8CCB` | Insight labels, links, information |
| Turnkey green | `#93C845` | Action, progress, proof emphasis |
| Cream | `#FBFAF6` | Editorial background and breathing room |
| Off-white | `#F4F2ED` | Secondary panels and paper texture |
| Charcoal | `#14161C` | Primary text |

The feed should be approximately 45% light, 30% photo-led, and 25% navy. Green is an accent, not a background default.

### Typography

- Primary family: Inter, matching the website.
- Production fallback: Helvetica Neue when rendering locally.
- Headline weight: 600–800.
- Body weight: 400–500.
- No more than three type sizes on a single static asset.
- Avoid all-caps paragraphs. All caps is reserved for labels of five words or fewer.
- Handwriting can appear as a small annotation only; never as the main headline.

### Composition

- Primary canvas: 1080 × 1350.
- Outer safe margin: 72–84 px.
- Keep faces, metrics, headlines, and logos inside a central 1080 × 1080 square-safe region whenever possible.
- Use asymmetric editorial compositions rather than centered-template repetition.
- Alternate dense and quiet tiles across the feed.
- Rounded corners and hairline borders should echo the website, but only where they support hierarchy.

## Photography direction

### Use more of

- Candid shop-owner and team moments
- Hands working, advisors talking, reports being reviewed
- Natural shop environments and imperfect details
- Window light, overhead shop light, real surface texture
- Tight crops that create a point of view
- Expressions that feel mid-conversation rather than posed

### Avoid

- Generic stock smiles and handshakes
- Glossy 3D marketing icons
- Fake dashboards or floating UI panels
- Overly cinematic luxury-car imagery
- Heavy blue overlays that hide the humanity of a photograph
- Photos with no narrative connection to the message

### Generated-image brief

When a missing scene must be generated:

- Use case: `photorealistic-natural`
- Documentary, candid, and unpolished
- Independent American repair shop environment
- Realistic fluorescent or window light
- Everyday clothing and believable shop texture
- No logos, fake text, fake data, or recognizable client identity
- Leave intentional negative space for the final deterministic headline

## Five design families

### 1. Documentary photo

Purpose: human connection, operator recognition, team credibility.

Structure: strong real image crop, 3–7 word headline, small label, minimal logo.

### 2. Proof card

Purpose: credibility and measurable outcomes.

Structure: one dominant metric, real report artifact or client identity, concise qualifier.

### 3. Editorial statement

Purpose: memorability and point of view.

Structure: 3–9 word statement, large type, generous negative space, one small graphic gesture.

### 4. Working note

Purpose: education and practical value.

Structure: photographed or drawn shop artifact, one takeaway, optional short annotation.

### 5. Service story

Purpose: consideration and conversion.

Structure: desired outcome first, service name second, one clear CTA. Avoid feature-list posters.

## Content strategy

The 30 assets form a ten-week campaign at three feed posts per week. Stories, short video, comments, and reposts should support the feed rather than forcing thirty consecutive publishing days.

### Weekly cadence

- Tuesday — shop-owner reality or useful insight
- Thursday — proof, testimonial, or human story
- Saturday — service story, process, or CTA

### Content mix

| Pillar | Posts | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Shop-owner reality | 7 | Stop the scroll through recognition |
| Education and useful guidance | 7 | Build authority and saves |
| Proof and testimonials | 7 | Build trust and reduce perceived risk |
| People and culture | 4 | Humanize Turnkey |
| Services and conversion | 5 | Create qualified next steps |

### Format mix

| Visual family | Assets |
|---|---:|
| Documentary photo | 10 |
| Proof card | 7 |
| Editorial statement | 6 |
| Working note | 4 |
| Service story | 3 |

## Feed rhythm

Avoid placing three typography-only posts, three proof posts, or three navy posts in a row. The target sequence is:

`photo → light editorial → proof → navy statement → photo → working note`

Repeat the rhythm with enough variation that the brand remains recognizable without becoming mechanical.

## CTA framework

- Awareness: no CTA or “Save this.”
- Education: “What are you tracking?” or “Send this to your manager.”
- Proof: “See more results.”
- Consideration: “See how Turnkey owns the process.”
- Conversion: “Book a consultation.”

Do not place a button-shaped CTA on every image. Most actions belong in the caption.

## Measurement plan

Track by pillar and visual family, not only by individual post:

- Saves and shares for educational posts
- Comments and profile visits for shop-reality posts
- Qualified website visits for proof and service posts
- Consultation starts for conversion posts
- Three-second view and completion rate for future video adaptations

After the first six weeks, keep the two strongest hooks from each pillar, retire the weakest design family, and build the next set from observed behavior rather than preference alone.

## V3 production brief

- Produce 30 new 1080 × 1350 masters in a separate `turnkey-30-v3` folder.
- Preserve v1 and v2 unchanged.
- Use supplied Turnkey photography wherever it supports the message.
- Generate only a small set of missing documentary scenes.
- Use captions and a ten-week calendar as part of the deliverable.
- Verify every asset at full size and as a small contact-sheet thumbnail.
