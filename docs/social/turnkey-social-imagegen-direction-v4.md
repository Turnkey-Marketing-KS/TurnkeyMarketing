# Turnkey Marketing Social Campaign V4 — Imagegen Direction

## What changed

V4 is an image-generated campaign system, not a photo-and-text template system. Built-in imagegen created the actual compositions: photographic treatment, collage, depth, typography staging, texture, motion, and visual metaphor. Code was used only after generation to make platform-safe 4:5 crops and assemble the contact sheet and calendar.

The creative north star is **independent auto-shop documentary × contemporary sports editorial × tactile campaign ephemera**.

## Campaign design rules

- Every tile must read as a complete social creative, not a slide or website section.
- Photography or a physical visual metaphor carries at least half of the idea.
- One short headline is the only planned on-image message.
- Condensed display typography is treated as image, with deliberate cropping and scale changes.
- Deep navy, warm cream, vivid blue, and acid-lime green create campaign recognition.
- Halftone, paper grain, torn edges, technical marks, route lines, and paint strokes add physical depth.
- Proof posts feel like milestones in motion, not metric cards.
- People posts preserve a candid, capable, independent-business tone.
- Service posts make the service tangible: a phone call, a mailpiece, a route, a key, a plan, or a full bay.
- No generic icon grids, rounded dashboard cards, laptop stock imagery, or fake social interfaces.

## Feed rhythm

The grid rotates among five visual jobs:

1. **Shop-owner tension** — operational pain shown inside a real shop.
2. **Human proof** — real clients and team members presented with editorial credibility.
3. **Milestone energy** — numbers integrated into automotive photography.
4. **Tactile service stories** — mail, planning, retention, and reporting shown as physical systems.
5. **Campaign statements** — short, high-impact ideas that reset the visual rhythm.

## Shared imagegen prompt framework

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: finished Instagram feed creative; portrait canvas; keep all essential content inside a centered 4:5 safe area
Primary request: Create a premium 2026 Turnkey Marketing social campaign post for independent auto repair shop owners.
Style/medium: contemporary automotive editorial advertising; authentic documentary photography; tactile print grain; art-directed rather than templated
Color palette: deep navy #172454, vivid blue #3294C8, acid lime #92CC3E, warm off-white #F4F1E9, charcoal
Typography: bold condensed uppercase grotesk; crisp, legible, intentionally scaled and cropped
Composition/framing: full-bleed or edge-to-edge; strong asymmetric hierarchy; depth; one clear focal point; small logo-safe corner
Constraints: render the supplied headline exactly once; no unrelated words, fake metrics, fake UI, invented logos, or watermark
Avoid: presentation slides, centered cards, generic Canva templates, stock-photo gloss, clip art, rounded dashboard cards
```

## Production and disclosure

- Generation mode: OpenAI built-in imagegen.
- CLI/API fallback: not used.
- The `generated-raw` folder contains the direct imagegen campaign masters.
- The `feed-4x5` folder contains the platform-ready 1080×1350 crops.
- Generated scenes and generated composites should not be represented as documentary proof of a specific client result unless the caption's claim is independently supported by Turnkey's source material.
- V4 intentionally preserves visible signs of art direction—grain, collage seams, type cropping, and halftone—rather than presenting generated imagery as untouched documentary photography.
