- `strategy.webp` — shop-specific marketing strategy
- `weekly-execution.webp` — weekly campaign execution
- `reporting.webp` — honest reporting and analysis
- `direct-mail.webp` — direct-mail marketing
- `digital-ads.webp` — digital advertising
- `local-seo.webp` — local SEO and map visibility
- `social-media.webp` — social-media marketing
- `customer-retention.webp` — customer retention
- `reviews.webp` — reviews and reputation
- `phone-calls.webp` — qualified phone calls
- `car-count.webp` — increased car count
- `revenue-growth.webp` — revenue growth
- `vendor-coordination.webp` — vendor coordination
- `consultation.webp` — marketing consultation

## Generation brief

Premium dimensional flat-illustration website icons with softly beveled layered shapes, crisp silhouettes, restrained highlights, and consistent visual weight. The palette uses Turnkey navy `#232E66`, key blue `#3F8CCB`, green `#93C845`, and small white highlights. Each icon contains one centered concept with no text, logo, watermark, frame, background scene, cast shadow, or reflection.
